## 5. Two findings the gate was not looking for

### 5.1 Pad velocity is dynamic, but the curve is compressed

Both captured strikes reported exactly `velocity 104`, which looked like a
fixed value. It is not: the daemon uses `PressureShape::Exponential(0.4)`
(`main.rs:1546`), and the Pi's `maschine.json` does not override it — that file
carries only `pad_notes`, `encoder_ccs` and `external_pad_leds`.

`pressure^0.4 * 127` compresses hard at the top:

| Pressure | Velocity |
|---|---|
| 0.2 | 66 |
| 0.5 | 96 |
| 0.6 | 103 |
| 1.0 | 127 |

So "a harder strike is louder" holds, but the usable range is roughly **66–127**,
not 1–127, and two similar strikes landing on the same number is ordinary. Worth
knowing before anyone reads a recorded take's flat velocities as a bug.

### 5.2 Unexplained: pressing REC emitted an encoder re-centre burst

At the REC press, lines 1140–1155 carry `controller 15, value 120` followed by
controllers 16–23 all at `value 64`. **64 is `ENC_CENTRE`** — that pattern is
`_recentre_encoders()`.

The first hypothesis, that CC 3 falls inside the group-button range and selected
a group, is **wrong**: `GROUP_CC_FIRST = 80`.

Most likely: CC 3 currently falls through unhandled — `_midi_event` returns
`False` for it — and something in Zynthian reacts. If so, **Task 7 fixes it as a
side effect**: once the driver binds CC 3 and returns `True`, nothing falls
through any more.

One sample, not proven. **Re-check at Task 11**: press REC with the new driver
loaded and confirm the encoder burst is gone.
