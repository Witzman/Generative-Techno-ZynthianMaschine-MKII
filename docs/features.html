    Every feature, and where it is written up

    This is the whole instrument in one list. Each row says what a feature does in a sentence and links to the page that walks you through it on the real hardware.

    It exists because the guide is organised by what you are doing — generating, playing, mixing, saving — which is the right shape for learning and the wrong shape for the question “does it do X?”. This page answers that question and nothing else.

    If something you expected is missing, it may be deliberate: What could come next carries the idea list and, at the end of it, what was considered and refused, with the reason.

    Generating patterns

    The generator owns the pattern. You set the odds and the shape; it writes the notes into the sequencer at every wrap.
