    Changelog

    What arrived, and when. Dates are the day the work landed on the instrument, not the day it was thought of — nothing appears here until it has been played on the hardware.

    Entries are written for a player: what you can now do that you could not do before. Fixes appear only where they changed what you would see or hear.

    Unreleased — the surface, redesigned

    Built and written up, but not yet played through. It is listed here rather than above so nobody reads it as shipped.

    The panel was rebuilt around four rules: four modes and a lens, one duration rule for every modifier, one alphabet for every light, and a home to come back to.

    The surface, redesigned

    • Four modes instead of five. CONTROL, STEP, AUTO, VOLUME — how it sounds, what it plays, what the machine does by itself, what holds for everything. The generator has a button of its own. Read
    • The lens — hold ALL and the eight encoders become whichever verb your hand last moved, on all eight channels. It replaces every page that existed for one verb. Read
    • HOME — press the big encoder and you are back at STEP, page 1, latches dropped. Nothing armed is cancelled. Read
    • One duration rule for every modifier — a tap latches, a hold is momentary, so you never have to decide which you meant first. Read
    • Every modifier lights, all saying the same five things: dark, dim, bright, a blink for latched, a pulse for counting down. FREEZE’s two depths are visible for the first time. Read
    • F1–F8 are mutes in every mode, CONTROL included. A plugin switch steps on its own column’s encoder. Read
    • The reroll buttons fire on a press rather than a long hold, and a second press before the bar takes it back. Read
    • ERASE + SELECT cancels everything armed, now that a tap on SELECT latches the macro pads. Read
    • RANGE reaches the drum CONTROL page — the kit walk’s window has a knob at last. Read

    Unreleased — what it can now do

    The same gate. These are new capabilities rather than changes to how the panel works.

    Everything else waiting for the same gate

    • RULE — a cellular automaton as a second way a register evolves, on both kinds. Read
    • LEAN — a third placement generator beside euclid, weighting hits toward the beat, the eighths or the offbeat. Read
    • LANE — how far the generators may stray, pruning the strays until a bar holds together. Read
    • MOVE — how often the machine may touch a channel at all, as a probability rather than a switch. Read
    • PHRASE and FILL — the fourth bar answers the first. Read
    • EXIT — a part leaves through its filter over a bar or four instead of stopping dead. Read
    • The bank grid — hold DUPLICATE and sixteen pads are sixteen whole arrangements. Read
    • A modulator’s shape, on the screen — a bound column draws a small picture of its wave. Read
    • It says when it has stopped — if generation stops, the indicator says so and the pads keep playing. Read
    • Per-screen brightness and contrast — two settings the daemon applies at start-up. Absent, nothing is read and nothing is written.
    • Half the display traffic — the two screens became independent, so a change on one no longer repaints the other.
    • A style as odds — an offline tool that expresses a snapshot pack as distributions and hard rules, and blends two styles without inventing values for the fields that cannot be averaged.

    31 August 2026 — eight features, gated the same evening

    • The GEN page for the three voices: ROTATE a melody, MODEL between the shift register and a bounded walk with SPAN and STRIDE, and FEED/AMOUNT so one voice drifts toward another.
    • The drum rhythm register on encoder 7 of the drum STEP page — steps appear and disappear without the line sliding. SWING moved one page along to its spread, where it reaches all eight.
    • A pad tap now flips a bit in the rhythm register on both kinds, so a hand-chosen step survives ROTATE and RHYTHM. HITS, DIVIDE and LENGTH are the way back.
    • The chord walker: the shared root steps along the scale every few bars.
    • The pump — MOD + RESTART snaps every modulator into the same phase.
    • Gate collapse on ARM: every note shortens into the landing and returns.
    • Pad pressure reaches the voices: squeeze a held pad and the filter opens.
    • MIDI export: a tool writes a snapshot’s eight patterns as a standard MIDI file.
    • Seven defects were found during that evening’s gate and six fixed the same night — among them ROTATE on a voice, which counted correctly on the display and never changed the sound.

    30 August 2026

    • The surface repaints itself after a replug. Before this, everything but the pads came back dark.
    • The pads stopped sticking, and a slow press sounds.

    22 August 2026

    • A new factory snapshot, with the previous one kept beside it as the way back.
    • A dropped pad light no longer stays wrong forever.

    21 August 2026

    • The factory snapshot ships at 125 BPM, which the sequencer can keep exactly — 124 drifts by a bar every eight minutes.
    • The phrase clock corrects itself against the sequencer, which took its drift from 3,896 parts per million to seven.
    • A held countdown says HELD, and a DROP with nobody nominated refuses out loud instead of muting all eight.
    • A drone and ambient snapshot pack.

    20 August 2026 — the performance package

    • The phrase clock and ARM: one bar count for the whole instrument, and gestures that land on a boundary.
    • DROP, BREAK, HALF and DOUBLE, the CHANCE ramp and the ratchet ramp.
    • The mute grid on MUTE, and beat repeat on the transport arrow.
    • The PENDING page, which says what is armed and how long it has left.
    • A master insert — one filter across the whole mix.
    • The moving sweep marker was removed: redrawing it was rebuilding both screens six times a second and taking the controller off the USB bus. What it said — how fast this is moving — became a word on the label instead.

    19 August 2026

    • Drift — the pattern itself moves at the wrap, and refuses on any channel you have taken over.
    • The big encoder became the page ring, so every mode can hold more than one page.
    • RATCHET, REROLL and the kit window.
    • A plugin’s switches reached the F row.
    • HITS and ROTATE were being stored and displayed without reaching the pattern. Fixed at the single write path rather than at the callers.

    18 August 2026

    • The genre pack: fifty ready-made arrangements.
    • A failing wrap was isolated to its own channel, so one bad channel can no longer take the instrument silent.

    16 August 2026

    • The voice generator split in two: MELODY and RHYTHM, so a line’s notes and its rests evolve separately.
    • Played steps on a voice draw amber, so you can see what is yours.
    • Encoder sensitivity halved, with TEMPO held restoring the old speed.

    15 August 2026

    • Navigation buttons stay lit, and the mode buttons go home.
    • REC lights only where recording is possible.

    14 August 2026

    • Modulation: hold MOD and an encoder sets how a value moves rather than what it is. Rates, shapes, depth, and MOD + ERASE to clear.

    13 August 2026 — the instrument becomes its own project

    • The driver, the daemon, the installer and the guide moved into one repository, self-contained, with the eight-channel instrument already playing: five euclidean drum channels and three Turing-machine voices.
